# Phase 1 Authentic Card Inventory (extracted from Thomson's First Hundred Hours guide)

Source: research/phase1.txt (local, copyrighted — this distilled inventory is our working reference).
Full structured JSON lives in the session transcript; key facts:

- ~814 specific words named across ~70 Rough-and-Ready Dozen sets, Meetings 1–40
- Target: ~150 words by Meeting 7 · 300+ by end of 1A (M15) · 1,000–1,200 by end of Phase 1
- Core artifact: the **Numbered Talking Picture Dictionary** — cumulative, sequentially
  numbered pictures (800+ by phase end) each with the nurturer's recording; two prints:
  one bound in order, one cut into cue cards
- Picture style: deliberately simple/amateur drawings, NO TEXT ever, localized per culture,
  real objects > toys > pictures; actions are PERFORMED (TPR), stick figures only as cues;
  "Photos of Us in Action" (photos of the actual learners/nurturer) is a recurring deck
