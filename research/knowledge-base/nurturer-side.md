## 1. The nurturer's role phase-by-phase (research corpus)

The through-line: the nurturer starts as a **playmate**, becomes a **storyteller**, then a **life-sharing mentor**, and ends as an **explainer/discourse coach** — "the nurturer plays with us less and less, and explains life to us more and more" (phase6). The helper is renamed **MENTOR from Phase 4 on** because communication is natural and no special training is needed. Nurturers are paid in Phases 1–5. One rule never changes: **host language only, never translate** — "otherwise the host person becomes the growing participator in YOUR languaculture: 'Let's keep the roles straight!'"

### Phase 1 — Playmate (NOT a teacher)
A host-world member who **plays** with the GPs in ways that let them understand.
- DOES: stays connected as a person, keeps it fun; speaks ONLY the host language in real sentences, as to social-equal friends; starts every new set with **two items, adds one at a time**; says each new word once (at most twice) then immediately questions; randomizes items and responders (question first, pause, then name); **weights questions toward newest/weakest words**; corrects errors naturally in host speech ("No, that's not the cow, that's the horse — where's the cow?"); drops back to two items for an overloaded GP and rebuilds; breaks long sentences into natural chunks; uses cue cards to keep ALL learned words in play and to decide instantly what to say next; records samples and the picture dictionary; demonstrates with puppets first; in talking games assists mid-utterance like a parent, **RECASTS errors** ("I hungry" → "Yes, you ARE hungry, I am hungry too"), repeats mispronounced words clearly in play (max ~3 retry attempts, no drills); may bring her own family photos (Meeting 7), deepening the real relationship.
- MUST NOT: translate or speak any other language during games; drill ("point-and-listen" repetition — a teacherly temptation); start with one or three items or add two at once; demand mastery, recall, or correct word forms; let GPs talk during listening games; allow writing; stall; expect story-perfect retellings.
- A **coach** (optionally present — a GP, beginner or advanced) silently trains the nurturer and never speaks during listening games except host-language encouragement; nurturers without coaches ideally get **a week of advance training**. Cautionary tale: a coach who unilaterally deemed possessives unimportant left GPs crippled in Phase 2 — "He should have asked us!!"

### Phase 2 — Responsive recaster → lead describer → story-sharer
- **2A (zero preparation needed)**: guesses what the grower is trying to say (pictures help), **recasts broken utterances into well-formed ones for echoing**, supplies words on request, numbers pictures for new words, records page descriptions using ONLY content actually discussed, runs listen-and-point quizzes repeating new words many times, and at book's end narrates the story past-tense in **three versions** (page-by-page detail; flowing while watching pages; freely from memory). Communication failures are noted for debriefing — never solved by switching to English. 2A activities double as orientation for any NEW nurturer.
- **2B (takes the lead)**: may take the picture book home overnight to understand it (**but must NOT script her speech — extemporaneous only**), describes pages fully while pointing, tells whole stories, answers clarification with monolingual explanations and example scenarios (the "in vain" sweeping example), builds **word–sentence–word vocabulary recordings** with meaning-revealing context sentences (growers must coach her on this format).
- **2C**: retells the grower's life stories naturally in past tense from stick-figure drawings, and shares her own life stories the same way.
- MUST NOT: translate everything into English; give grammar lectures or long culture explanations in a shared language; plan/memorize speech; act like a "teacher" during GP retellings (retelling is at the GROWER's level with conversational assistance only). Her "misinterpretations" of pictures are host-world perspective to be welcomed — only plot-breaking errors (mouse called a dog) warrant pushback.

### Phase 3 — Paid storytelling companion (ideal: loves storytelling AND can gear stories to the grower's level)
"Kindly, gently, patiently" meets the grower in his growth zone.
- DOES: learns **Bridge Stories** at home (reads a version in a language she knows 2–3 times) and retells them spontaneously in her own words, culturally adapted (host-style openings/closings, "angel" for "fairy") without gutting the plot; tells/reads stories slowly for recording; explains unknown words/sentences IN THE HOST LANGUAGE ONLY — definitions, examples, demonstrations, pantomime, posing ("acting lazy"), stern voices, example floods ("A doctor made me take medicine. My mother made me get water…"); confirms or gently recasts trial sentences; **writes every new word into the word log**; authors all **Scripts of Life** (only a host person can produce a genuine host-viewpoint script) and expands them when probed; narrates action cartoons live with the screen hidden, then retells while deliberately re-including word-log items; leads Reminiscing about Shared Experiences; keeps growing idea lists for Scripts of Life and Shared Experiences; reads written stories aloud (and retells written texts in the spoken register for diglossic languages); retells the grower's life stories for clean Listening Library recordings; records herself telling stories native-to-native (3C); suggests Lexicarry bubble options; **introduces the grower to her own friends** to seed relationships partly independent of her.
- MUST NOT: use the grower's language or translate; let sessions dissolve into hours of small talk (plateau risk — small talk opens the day and arises out of activities, never replaces them); assume host-world background knowledge too early (that's why Bridge Stories come first); radically alter plots; drill arbitrary grammar; **sit passively while the GP reads aloud** (a waste of paid time — her reading to HIM is the valuable direction); let the GP read along while listening; keep difficulty static — it must keep rising; be the grower's ONLY relationship by 3B.

### Phase 4 — MENTOR (renamed; no training needed; paid a reasonable local hourly rate)
Mentors just interact at an understandable level — they instinctively simplify, which keeps recordings re-listenable.
- DOES: tells their life story and daily-life accounts at whatever length and in whatever direction **they** choose; pauses on demand to clarify words, idioms, pronunciation, cultural background; brief explanations in clarification, full explanations in expansions; answers grand-tour/mini-tour/experience/structural/contrast questions; helps fill and name lists of life; re-describes and interprets observed scenes; judges every Record-Yourself-for-Feedback sentence ("would a host person say that?") and supplies the native version; discusses each word-log entry in the host language for vocabulary recordings; corrects gently while helping the grower formulate sentences live.
- The grower must NOT steer the storyteller "back on track" (there is no track but theirs), ask about anything not yet mentioned, push painful topics early, substitute their own speculations for the mentor's interpretations, or break **physician/lawyer/priest-level confidentiality**: never replay recordings without permission, erase on request, retell only anonymized non-personal anecdotes (a miscarriage is never repeated in any identifying way).
- Expected by-product: a strong personal bond reliably forms with anyone who shares their life story at this depth — **that bond is the actual point of the phase**. A separate "language learning advisor" figure helps plan time allocation and life-domain restructuring.

### Phase 5 — MENTOR as insider-view clarifier (typically paid for clarifying meetings)
- DOES: real small talk as a real relationship; listens to native-media segments with the GP; repeats rapid/garbled phrases slowly and clearly; elaborates each new word conversationally with contexts, examples, register notes ("it sounds fancy — they're on national television"); explains puzzling stretches and whole areas of host life; answers expanding/descriptive/domain-of-life questions with personal opinions and digressions; gives simpler native-to-non-native summary paraphrases; retells the GP's stories in a more host-like way; gives feedback on the GP's recorded speech; writes word lists if literate; reads printed texts aloud naturally for recording; supplies the "insider's view" on movie scenes.
- Separate host people (not necessarily the mentor) make native-to-native source recordings — **always WITHOUT the GP present** (a GP audience de-nativizes them).
- MUST NOT: reduce word help to bare definitions (elaboration must be coaxed); let clarifying become mechanical ("What does that word mean? OK, next bit"); have the GP read aloud to her; let the GP follow a transcript while listening; demand polished grammar; ever use translation.

### Phase 6 — Explainer, discourse coach, subject-matter professor
Ideal higher-level nurturer (Leaver's model): a content instructor who does NOT know the grower's language, has never taught their own language, treats the grower like a local student, but sees communication growth as central.
- DOES: brainstorms discourse lists / needs analysis; reviews Record-for-Feedback attempts, tracking every needed improvement while negotiating meanings; records the **IMPROVED version of the grower's own text — pointedly NOT her own spontaneous version** (a version founded on the grower's stays in the growth zone; a fully spontaneous native version "may not feed your growth as much"); in B-stages models the discourse from scratch for massaging and later retelling; narrates scripts-of-life in small steps; plays both roles in role-plays; accompanies the grower into real venues and leads Phase 3-style reminiscing afterward; reads the same materials as the grower and discusses them on recording. Selection matters: **"all nurturers are not created equal"** — pick one eloquent in the target discourse, not a cliché-producer.
- MUST NOT: translate; carry on unstructured everyday chat (paying for "the same sort of unstructured conversation" the grower already has with the house helper is wasted money); listen to the grower read aloud; hand over spontaneous native versions detached from the grower's attempts; drift out of the 5 special-growth characteristics.

### Cross-cutting (graphics corpus)
The nurturer drives every activity in the target language only: names pictures while pointing with expressive voice/gesture, acts emotions with face and body, issues ever-richer pointing/manipulation commands, shuffles known and new cards to calibrate difficulty, role-plays register with dolls/puppets, demonstrates quantifiers with seeds and candy, records segments for home review. Must NOT translate, must NOT require or pressure speech (pointing IS the expected response for new material), must NOT turn sessions into grammar explanation, and should not insist on a single "correct" sentence per social function.

## 2. Session structure phase-by-phase (what the nurturer runs)

### Phase 1
- Meetings **2–3 hours**; plans decoupled from meetings (stop mid-plan between games, resume next time). ~2 plans/day × 5 days/week finishes 40 plans in ~4 weeks. Players: 1 nurturer, 2–6 GPs, optional silent coach.
- **1A shape**: chain of 4–8 games — Rough-and-Ready Dozens (new word sets), LISTEN & DO action games, recombination/strengthening games (Make It True, cue-card stacks), Photos of Us in Action, Cartoon Bubbles — each game ends with a **~1–2 minute recorded sample**; day closes with the **Numbered Talking Picture Dictionary recording**.
- **1B fixed skeleton**: (1) "Day and Time" opener; (2) ~10 min sound-focus games (Hear the Difference?, Sorting Sounds, dictation); (3) Cartoon Bubbles/Lexicarry; (4) a major TALKING activity re-using the PREVIOUS meeting's new words (ladder game, info gap, role play); (5) new-vocabulary LISTENING games (words meet listening at least one meeting before talking); (6) form-focused/structured-input games as needed.
- GPs silent during listening games; homework = listen to all samples while pointing/acting, refresh picture dictionary, prepare materials.
- (Graphics corpus, inferred): ~30 numbered meeting plans, each ≈2 h, built from 5–15-minute games with envelope card sets; night-before staging basket prep; after-session re-living with audio + master sheets.

### Phase 2
- **2A**: 5–10 min small talk (learn one new fact about the nurturer, share one back) → repeating ~55-min cycle: refresh previous pages + **monolingual half hour** describing picture pages (timer set; lengthens to 60–90 min over time) → optional 5-min shared-language debrief from failure notes → ~10-min page-by-page recording of what was discussed → ~10 min listen-and-point/act-out + summary recording; cycle repeats, tea breaks when weary. Steps 6–7 (three past-tense versions; GP retelling) fire only at book's end.
- **2B**: small talk → 10 min nurturer describes new pages while pointing (recorded) → **30–80 min clarifying the recording monolingually** → optional debrief → 15 min word-sentence-word vocab recording → 5–10 min listen-and-point + sample. Later 2B inverts: nurturer tells the whole story past-tense FIRST (10+ min, recorded), then clarification fills the meeting.
- **2C cycle**: small talk → 30–70 min grower tells a life story while drawing → 10 min nurturer retells it past-tense (recorded) → 15 min vocab recording → 10 min listen-and-point + recording.
- Throughout: **20–25 meeting-hours/week**; debrief windows shrink toward "monolingual days" and "monolingual weeks"; homework = repeated listening to the day's recordings.

### Phase 3
- Sessions 2–5 hours (ideal **25 h/week over 5 days** + ~15 h/week solo listening/reading). EVERY session opens with **10–15 min small talk in the host language** — relationship first.
- **Canonical 3A day**: small talk → Reminiscing about yesterday's Shared Experience (1 h) → Strengthening Vocabulary (20 min, strictly capped) → **Bridge Story recording + massaging (2.5 h — the mainstay, ≥ half of all time)** → Script of Life (1 h) → Action Cartoon (1 h) OR Shared Experience outing. Simpler variant: small talk + vocab + 3.5–4.5 h Bridge Story + Scripts of Life.
- **3B sample day**: pre-session re-listen to the translation of today's host story → 15 min small talk → 15 min vocab → 2 h recording/massaging a host story → 15 min Input Flooding → 15 min role-play → 1 h Familiar-Place Description → 1 h starting conversations with strangers in the park (or visiting befriended neighbours). Evening: re-listen to today's recordings, next week's story translation, review idea lists.
- **Part-time pattern**: 2 h every morning — Bridge Stories 4 days/week, Scripts of Life 1 day/week, small talk daily — with fanatical Listening Library use in every spare moment.
- Quality bar regardless of mix: speech understandable with only moderate massaging, **8+ new words/hour**, lots of interaction, lots of grower talking. Everything recorded, massaged, re-listened until easy.

### Phase 4
- **No fixed lesson template** — natural recorded conversations structured by recursive activity cycles. Canonical Life Story / Walk-of-Life cycle per topic: (1) one broad open question, record, stay almost silent (tellings 2–20+ min); (2) clarification session replaying the recording, pausing at every gap (Two-Recorder Technique merges this with expansion-asking, itself recorded); (3) word-recording segment per new word; (4) plan expansions on the tracking form; (5) record expansions, recycle recursively; (6) solo re-listening 1–2+ times within days; (7) retell/summary back to the mentor.
- Detailed Observation alternates solo fieldwork with a mentor session (grower describes exhaustively, mentor interprets, mentor records a summary) + triangulation mini-conversations with 2–3 other hosts.
- Monitoring: every 4 days check new-words-per-hour; if well under 8/h, dedicate the 5th day to hole-finding / Phase 2–3 vocabulary activities. Macro-allocation example: 300 h Life Story (5 people) + 100 h Walk-of-Life (5 roles) + 100 h Observe-and-Describe.

### Phase 5
- Typical supercharged meeting = **2 hours**: (1) small talk 5–10 min ("What are you thinking about?" — wait five seconds); (2) GP retells in own words yesterday's clarified portion; (3) **clarifying the recording 80–90 min** — new 5–10-min segment, then loop bit by bit: slow repetitions, per-word mini-conversations, explanations, grand-tour/mini-tour expansion, optional simpler retelling; (4) supercharged talking activities 20–30 min (picture discussion, opinion defense, teaching a topic).
- Same evening: re-listen to the clarified portion + word recording, rotate older Listening Library items. ~Two-thirds of weekly supercharged time goes to clarifying. Each meeting hour consumes ~20 min of fresh recording → a 25 h/week learner needs **~5 h of fresh recordings weekly**.

### Phase 6
- **No prescribed curriculum** — principles + examples only. (1) Weekly lifestyle budget: of ~112 waking hours, well over 10% (ideally 20%+) lived in the host world; Phase-6 people optionally add ~5 h/week special-growth activities; Phase un-6 people need many hours (2–3 h/week "won't work" — say so plainly). (2) **Discourse-mastery sequence** per communication situation: Prelude (Phase-1-style vocab fill) → Version A, GP in the lead (perform/record → Record-for-Feedback review with the nurturer → record nurturer's improved version of the GROWER's text → Listening Library) for 10–20 h → Version B, nurturer in the lead (nurturer performs/records → grower massages → retells in own words, never verbatim) → optional Scripts-of-Life → role-play with role switching → real shared experience in the venue → Phase-3 Reminiscing afterwards. (3) Block targets: 100 h (low level) or 300 h (Terminal 1+/2+) of five-characteristic activities to produce a "new you"; 20–30 h buys growth in one specific area. (4) Homework: Listening Library, massage recordings, extensive reading, challenging broadcasts.

## 3. What the app's nurturer hub currently offers (`/nurture`, 848 lines)

Self-description in the header comment: *"GPA's hard part is knowing what to DO in a session: this page is the meeting-plan book an untrained native speaker never has to read."* Card words are host-language only (image + audio carry meaning; "show word" is off by default — ears before eyes); curriculum text stays plain English like the printed phase guides.

**Access gates (in order):**
1. `profile.role === "grower"` → **GrowerGate** ("The nurturer's bench"): reach Phase 6 or flip role in onboarding to unlock — "The circle closes when you nurture the next grower through their first hundred hours." Links: See Phase 6 (`/courses/ever-growing`) and Flip my role (`/onboarding`).
2. `profile.nurturerCertStatus !== "passed"` → **TrainingGate**: "Every nurturer walks through the golden rules and answers a short check first — about five minutes." Shows a retry note if `nurturerCertStatus === "failed"`. CTA → `/nurture/training`.

**Header**: "Nurturer Studio" ("You don't need training — you need this bench… everything a playmate needs, nothing a teacher would") + a **nurture-language picker** built from `profile.nurtureLangs` (falling back to `targetLang`). Languages without full content decks borrow Spanish (`FULL_CONTENT_LANGS` fallback).

**a) Rule Zero + six golden rules** (also the training curriculum; exact English text from i18n):
- **Rule Zero (highlighted, "the only unbreakable one")**: *Speak ONLY your language* — "Never translate — not even one word. Pictures and your hands carry the meaning. Your grower stands outside a wall of noise, and every sentence you speak in your own language opens a window in it."
- ✌️ **Start with two, add one at a time** — never one, never three; say a new word once, at most twice, then straight back to asking in random order.
- 🎯 **Ask the newest, weakest word most often** — weight questions toward the wobbly; if someone overloads, drop back to two cards and rebuild.
- 🔁 **Recast, never correct** — "No, that's the horse… where's the cow?" — errors fixed naturally inside the play, never as a lesson.
- 👉 **Never force speech** — pointing IS the answer in Phase 1; comprehension first, talking emerges on its own.
- 🧸 **You are a playmate, not a teacher** — stay connected, keep the game fast, laugh a lot.
- 🎙️ **Record the last two minutes** — a talking picture dictionary over today's cards so the grower can re-live the meeting at home.

**b) Session Planner** — deterministic plan composer:
- Pick the grower's **phase (1–6)** and meeting length (**30 / 45 / 60 min**); a seeded PRNG (`mulberry32`) + shuffle button produce a reproducible plan.
- Every plan = fixed **Warm-up review (5′)** ("Recycled cards from last time… Every old word stays in play forever") + 2–4 activities drawn from `phase.activities` in `src/lib/phases.ts` (2 for 30′, 3 for 45′, 4 for 60′), with minutes distributed proportionally to each activity's own weight (min 4′) + fixed **Closing recording (3′)** (Talking Picture Dictionary). Minute math strip proves the plan sums exactly.
- Each activity block shows its `how` text, a kind tag (listening/speaking/vocabulary/literacy/culture/conversation with emoji), and an **"open in app →"** link when the activity has a `practiceHref`.

**c) Card Table** — projector-style big-card kit ("Point your screen at the camera — one giant picture card at a time"):
- 10 vocab domains from `VOCAB_DOMAINS` (food, animals, home, body, traveling, family, sport, health, nature, work); shuffle-deck button (seeded); prev/next card, card counter.
- Big emoji card with the written word **hidden by default** ("👂 ears before eyes"); an Eye toggle reveals it.
- 🔊 button speaks the word via TTS; a violet pill speaks the **"Where is …?" power question** — hardcoded per language (`¿Dónde está…?`, `Где…?`, `Où est… ?`, `Wo ist…?`, `Onde está…?`, `Dov'è…?`, `Kote…?`).
- Footer tip: "Name it. Point. Ask '¿Dónde está…?'. Shuffle in old cards — newest and weakest most often."

**d) Meeting Timer** — the GPA meeting shape as a segmented 30-minute clock: **5′ Review · 20′ Play · 5′ Record**. At each segment boundary the timer speaks a short cue in the nurture language ("¡A jugar!" / "¡A grabar!" / "¡Listo!" — cue sets for es/ru/fr/de/pt/it/ja/zh/ht). Big countdown, per-segment progress bars, start/pause/resume/reset, demo ×60 fast-forward behind env flags. Footnote: "At each boundary the timer calls the next segment out loud — in your language, of course."

**Roster & forum seed data** (`src/lib/nurturers.ts`): 17 demo nurturers across es/ru/fr/de/pt/it/ja/zh/en/ht — deliberately ordinary people (retired baker, tour guide, nurse, taxi driver, florist, fishmonger, kindergarten helper, bus driver, seamstress, school-lunch cook, train conductor, street-food vendor, mahjong grandmother, tea-shop owner, school-bus driver, Iron-Market machann, taptap driver) with marketplace fields: `region` (dialect headline), `ratePerHourUsd` (PPP-scaled, $4–14), `methodCertified` (10 of 17), `exchangeOpen` (time-for-time), `growersNurtured`, `phasesGuided` (mostly "1–2"; max "3–4"). `FORUM_SEED` includes nurturer voice posts modeling method fidelity ("speak MORE, not slower-and-broken"; "I never correct in conversation — I just say it back the right way"). `nurturerI18n.ts` localizes roster bio/region/tags via keys (`nur_{id}_bio` etc.), guarding the AI nurturer "Nuri" (id `"ai"`) from double-keying.

## 4. The training/certification flow as built (`/nurture/training`)

A one-time golden-rules walkthrough + quiz gating the Nurturer Studio (entry: TrainingGate). Growers are redirected away. Three steps:

1. **Walkthrough** — the SAME six golden rules as the hub, one card at a time (emoji, title, body, "N / 6" progress, Back/Next), ending with "I'm ready — start the check".
2. **Quiz** — six multiple-choice questions, all answered before submit, **pass = 5 of 6** (`PASS_THRESHOLD = 5`), unlimited retries:
   - Q1: how many times to say a brand-new word → "Once or twice, then straight back to asking".
   - Q2: overwhelmed grower → "Drop back to two cards and rebuild" (distractor: "Switch to English and explain").
   - Q3: wrong card pointed → "Naturally recast it in the moment… never as a formal correction".
   - Q4: grower hasn't spoken after a few sessions → "No — pointing IS the answer in Phase 1; speech emerges later on its own".
   - Q5: your role → "A playmate — staying connected, keeping the pace fast, and laughing a lot".
   - Q6: why record the last two minutes → "So the grower has a talking picture dictionary of today's cards to relive at home".
3. **Result** — pass: "You're ready to nurture! Golden rules unlocked — welcome to the bench" → Studio; fail: "Not quite yet… no limit on attempts" → Review & retry (restarts the walkthrough).

**Completion recording** — `updateProfile` writes onto the local profile (typed in `src/lib/types.ts` lines 100–109):
- `nurturerCertStatus`: `"not_started" | "passed" | "failed"` (a fail never erases a previous `nurturerCertPassedAt`)
- `nurturerCertScore`: last score 0–100 · `nurturerCertAttempts`: incremented pass or fail · `nurturerCertPassedAt`: ISO date of most recent pass · `nurturerCertMissed`: ids of missed questions (for owner review)

CloudProfileBridge syncs the profile blob to Convex; the site owner reviews attempts via `/admin/nurturers` backed by `profiles.listNurturerCertifications` (section 6). Note the quiz certification (`nurturerCertStatus`) is separate from the roster's cosmetic `methodCertified` badge on demo nurturers.

## 5. What the nurturer does in the live session room (`/session`, human mode)

The room implements the authentic Phase-1 **Dirty Dozen** flow: *"the NURTURER controls everything the grower sees. Card 1 is free (INTRO → ECHO); every further card is earned through a review round of 'Where is …?' questions, and every 3rd card the round sweeps ALL introduced cards."* Two modes share the identical state machine (`src/lib/sessionFlow.ts`): HUMAN (nurturer drives from a nurturer-only tray) and AI (Nuri, the language sibling, runs the same paced flow with speech). Currently **single-device**: both partners share one screen; `CONVEX_SYNC` comments mark where two-device real-time sync will plug in ("real-time remote role/deck sync between two devices ships with the Convex backend").

**Grower's view (the "grower stage")**: only the introduced cards, as image/emoji tiles — **the written word is never shown to the grower**. Status strip shows listen/tap prompts, review progress ("2/4", "big round — every card!"), and on a miss the iceberg message: a miss isn't a failure — "It's in your iceberg… the nurturer says it once more". During intro: "New card — just listen. No pressure to speak." Taps are only enabled during review.

**Nurturer's tray (human mode only, amber-bordered, tagged "🤫 nurturer only")**:
- **Whole-deck view**: all 12 cards with their **written words visible**, un-introduced cards dimmed, the current review target highlighted amber, the newest card highlighted violet.
- **Cue box — literally what to say aloud right now**:
  - INTRO: "Say it twice" — `Listen. «word» … One more time. «word»` (cue words per language from `CUES`: ru/en/ht/ja sets — Слушай/Ещё раз/Покажи/Молодец; Koute/Ankò/Montre m/Bravo…), plus a 🔊 TTS button so the nurturer can hear pronunciation.
  - REVIEW: "Ask aloud — question k/n (· full sweep)" — `Point to it. «Where is the cow?»`, with the ANSWER shown ("Answer: 🐄 cow — highlighted in your tray"); after a grower miss: "**Re-say it slowly**: One more time. «cow»".
  - ROUND DONE: praise cue («Молодец!») + "next card unlocked". DECK DONE: run free review rounds or end.
- **Tray controls**: 🃏 **Reveal next card** (disabled until the owed review is satisfied — a progress ring fills as questions are asked; "🔒 review first — the next card is earned"); 🔁 **Review round** (badge shows planned length: k = min(introduced, 4) weighted toward newest/weakest via a squared-recency `pickReviewTarget`, or a full shuffled sweep of ALL cards on every 3rd card); **Switch roles** (two-step modal: confirm → pick the new deck language; the grower becomes the nurturer of THEIR strong language; the fresh language restarts at Meeting 1 — "a fresh language starts at the wall of noise").
- **Rotating joke prompts** every 18 s ("GPA: laughter makes words stick"): hide a card behind your back; give the animal a silly voice; put a card on your head and ask for it with a straight face; point at the wrong card yourself, look very confused; whisper the word… then say it like an opera singer; etc. (8 prompts in `JOKE_PROMPTS`).

**Shared room mechanics**: countdown timer is the single source of truth for session end (booked `?duration=`, default 30 min) — when the 12-card deck completes early the room auto-loops into full review sweeps rather than idling; half-time banner nudges the role switch; points (⭐) awarded per correct tap; "🪴 X nurtures / 🌱 Y grows" role tags; wavebar "speaking" shimmer. **Recording ("talking picture dictionary")**: consent-first — nothing records until everyone in the room accepts the ConsentDialog (consent remembered for THIS session only, deliberately never persisted); mic or mic+camera; clip playback + download at session end with the re-living instruction ("point at the cards while you listen"). Booking params (`?nurturer=&activity=&duration=`) come from /schedule; beta builds only allow the AI nurturer unless `NEXT_PUBLIC_ENABLE_SEEDED_NURTURERS` is set — a guessed ID is never turned into an apparent live human. Session end logs `completeActivity(minutes, points)`.

## 6. Backend support (Convex)

`grep -l nurturer convex/*.ts` → only `profiles.ts` and `schema.ts`; requests/calls/credits are nurturer-adjacent (the exchange marketplace rails).

- **`schema.ts`** — no dedicated nurturer table. `profiles` mirrors the localStorage Profile: structured fields (clerkId, name, role as `"grower" | "nurturer"` string, targetLang, knownLangs, immersion, hoursListened, phase) + a lossless `data: v.any()` blob that carries everything else, **including `nurtureLangs` and all five `nurturerCert*` fields**. Other tables: `blocks` (directional community blocks), `safetyReports` (fail-closed moderator allowlist), `waitlist`, **`wallets`** (two currencies: `exchangeHours` — free growing hours *earned by nurturing* and spent to be grown — plus `paidHours` for the PPP-scaled paid marketplace), **`ledger`** (append-only credit history with partner/lang/minutes), **`requests`** (Tandem-style session requests), `messages` (1:1 text + voice notes), **`calls` + `iceCandidates`** (WebRTC 1:1 signalling — Convex relays SDP/ICE only, media is P2P over Google STUN), `parties` + `partyGuests` (group sessions).
- **`profiles.ts`** — `upsertProfile` (pushes the localStorage profile up unchanged); `getProfile`; **`listNurturerCertifications`** — admin-only roster (owner-email allowlist from `NURILANG_ADMIN_EMAILS`, fail-closed) of every non-grower profile with name, role, nurtureLangs, certStatus/score/attempts/passedAt/missed, powering `/admin/nurturers` "so the site owner can spot-check readiness after the fact rather than gating live sign-ups on manual review"; `listPeople` — privacy-preserving exchange roster for /world (city-level only, opaque doc ids, never Clerk ids/emails; includes `nurtureLangs`, exchange flag, certificates, photo; honors blocks).
- **`requests.ts`** — `sendRequest` (opaque profile id resolved server-side; rate-limited to 20/hour; refreshes an existing pending request instead of duplicating; blocked-pair checks), `respondRequest` (accept/decline, recipient-only), `myIncoming` / `myOutgoing`. All behind a `communityExchangeEnabled()` feature flag.
- **`calls.ts`** — `startCall` requires the callee's `data.exchange` to be true ("This person is not open to calls"), 10-second ringing rate limit, SDP size caps; participant-scoped answer/ICE/status mutations.
- **`credits.ts`** — deliberately disabled: `recordEntry` **throws** "Direct credit entries are disabled. Credits require a verified session or payment event." A browser-supplied amount is not money; the earn-by-nurturing loop awaits trusted session-completion/payment workflows.

Net: certification state syncs (inside the profile blob) and is admin-queryable; person-to-person requests, messaging, and video-call signalling exist; but there are **no nurturer-session records, no booking table (bookings live in the profile blob), no live session-state channel yet (the designed `sessionEvents` channel is unbuilt), and no working credit-earning path**.

## 7. Gaps: what the methodology says nurturers need that the app doesn't yet give them

**Phase coverage is the headline gap.** Every purpose-built nurturer tool — golden rules, quiz, card table, timer cues, Dirty-Dozen tray — is Phase 1 material. The Session Planner nominally spans phases 1–6, but it only re-skins each phase's activity list into time blocks; it doesn't teach the nurturer *how* to run any post-Phase-1 activity. ROADMAP item 5 concedes this: "Phase 2–6 activity depth — currently strongest in Phase 1."

Per phase:
- **Phase 1**: No cue-card tracking of ALL learned words across meetings — the research's core nurturer skill ("uses cue cards to keep ALL learned words in play and to decide instantly what to say next") has no per-grower word history; the deck is derived only from `hoursLogged` → meeting number. No coach role (the research's silent nurturer-trainer). No support for LISTEN & DO / talking-game/recast guidance beyond one golden rule. The timer hard-codes 30 minutes (5/20/5) and the planner caps at 60 — the research meeting is 2–3 hours with 4–8 games; the 1B fixed skeleton (day/time opener, sound-focus games, prior-meeting talking activity, listening-before-talking ordering) isn't encoded.
- **Phase 2**: Nothing for the nurturer's actual 2A/2B/2C jobs — recasting broken utterances for echo, numbered picture recordings restricted to discussed content, monolingual-half-hour timing, word–sentence–word vocabulary recordings (the format growers must coach her on), three-version past-tense narration, debrief-note capture, or new-nurturer orientation via 2A activities.
- **Phase 3**: No Bridge Story take-home prep flow (read 2–3×, retell spontaneously — never scripted), no massaging-loop UI (ROADMAP P3), no Scripts-of-Life authoring tool, no word log the nurturer writes into, no action-cartoon narration mode (screen hidden from grower), no idea lists, no Listening Library the nurturer records into, no "difficulty must keep rising" calibration, no anti-small-talk session shape.
- **Phase 4**: No mentor-facing tooling at all — life-story interview flow, two-recorder technique (ROADMAP P4), expansion tracking forms, per-word recording segments, and crucially **no confidentiality guidance** (the research demands physician-level trust norms for recorded life stories; the app's consent dialog covers recording consent, not retention/replay/anonymization ethics). The nurturer→mentor terminology shift is absent.
- **Phase 5**: No clarifying-meeting scaffold (segment loop, word-elaboration prompts against bare-definition drift), no mechanism for native-to-native source recordings made WITHOUT the grower present, no mentor feedback flow on grower recordings.
- **Phase 6**: No Record-for-Feedback loop where the nurturer records the improved version *of the grower's own text* (the phase's signature move), no discourse-selection/needs-analysis tool, no eloquence-based nurturer matching ("all nurturers are not created equal" — the roster's `phasesGuided` tops out at "3–4" and isn't used for matching).
- **Training**: one 6-question Phase-1 quiz vs. the research's "week of advance training", ongoing coach supervision, and phase-specific orientations (2A as role orientation, 2B for clarifying recordings, one-time Scripts-of-Life training). No phase-2+ certification exists, yet nothing stops a Phase-1-certified nurturer from being booked at any phase.
- **Session infrastructure**: recordings are device-only downloads — there's no way for the nurturer to deliver recordings into the grower's Listening Library, which the methodology makes the nurturer's daily output from Phase 2 onward. Two-device live sessions, real bookings, group sessions (the original GPA context is group-based — 2–6 GPs per nurturer), and the earn-by-nurturing credit loop all await backend work already sketched (sessionEvents channel, wallets/ledger, disabled `recordEntry`).
- **Quality bar**: the 8-new-words-per-hour target and the every-4-days rate check (Phase 4) aren't surfaced to nurturers anywhere.
- **Marketplace honesty**: roster fields (`ratePerHourUsd`, `methodCertified`, `growersNurtured`) are demo-seeded with no backing systems; the grammar-teacher tier (`teacher: true`, bookable at phase ≥ 4) is roadmap-only.
