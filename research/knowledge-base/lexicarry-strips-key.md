This addendum resolves the gap in two parts, because **there are two distinct numbering systems** that earlier reports blurred together:

1. **The homemade "Lexicarry-like" strips** drawn by Angela Thomson in the Graphics Packet (pp. 4–10), labeled **1A–20** (38 drawn frames), whose text key is the 2-page `Lexicarry-like strips-Key.pdf/.docx`. The Key covers frames **1A–19 only** (37 keyed frames); drawn frame **20 is not in the Key** (see §3).
2. **The commercial Lexicarry book's numbered functions (1)–(57)**, cited function-by-function in the Phase 1 meeting plans (`phase1.txt`). This is where the "~57" in phase1.md §"~57 numbered Lexicarry functions" comes from — and the count is **exactly 57** (see §4).

## 5. Method text from the Graphics Packet intro (p. 3, "A Set of Lexicarry-Like Pictures Created by Angela Thomson")

Key content of the full-page intro (extracted verbatim from the PDF text layer):
- Lexicarry described: "story strips similar to comic strips, with about three frames per story. The 'bubbles' … are left empty. The stories illustrate approximately sixty common language functions and communication situations."
- The packet's strips are a substitute when the book is hard to get: activities "can be done with picture strips like in Lexicarry or with the sample strips provided for you here or with picture strips that you draw yourselves [often representing local people and situations more appropriately]."
- Month-1 (listening-only) usage: "The Nurturer begins by telling us what each person might be saying in the stories and then asks us questions like 'Who is saying, "May I help you?"; who is saying, "I'm sorry"?'. We respond by pointing, not by speaking. In a few moments … we can recognize ten new 'survival expressions'."
- **Four factors that vary the form of each function**: (a) "One's social standing relative to the person one is talking to," (b) "How well one knows the person," (c) "Who is listening," (d) "The circumstances under which the communication occurs." The GP "should not expect to simply memorize a single sentence for each function" — first pass = one utterance per bubble; later passes = discuss options; eventually role-play, incl. "have your nurturer role-play with the old man doll and the boy doll greetings from younger to elder."
- **Ordering rule**: "You do not need to do these sets of pictures in a specific order [except for doing those with the same number together]. However, we recommend a 'logical' order. E.g. Don't do asking prices or asking time until you actually understand numbers (Meeting plan 12 and following)."

## 8. Anomalies and editorial defects in the sources (worth preserving verbatim)

1. **"(6)" typo in MP16** (phase1.txt line 1743): "(10) to offer and refuse food, (11) to offer and accept help, (6) to request and accept help" — the third item should be **(12)**; (6) was already assigned to "interrupting a conversation" (MP4), and p. 4 must hold strips 10–12 for the 57-count to close (it does).
2. **MP27 header misprint**: `Meeting Plan 27, Game 2: "Cartoon Bubbles" Lexicarry,  p. 13Lexicarry, p. 14` — concatenation typo; content is p. 14.
3. **MP30 and MP31 both cite p. 17**; MP30's game introduces no new numbered functions (it is the why-explanation variation), so p. 17's new functions (52)–(54) actually land in MP31.
4. **Duplicated concepts in the book list**: toasting at (40) and (46); fear at (33) and (47). Not errors — different strips/situations — but an app deduplicating by concept would collapse 57 to ~54.
5. **Key repeats one description**: frames 6 and 7 are both "Expression of pleasure or gratefulnes[s]" (two different drawings, one function).
6. **Key stops at 19; drawings go to 20** (celebration scene, §3). materials-assets.md's "numbered 1A–20" is right for the drawings; its "KEY for strips 1A–19" is right for the text. phase1.md's single sentence merging them ("enumerates strips 1A–20 … celebration (20)") slightly overstates what the Key text contains.
7. **"Lexicary" misspelling** at phase1.txt line 2799 (p. 78/75 reference), and "miriad" in the Key front matter — quoted as-is above.
8. The 2-page Key has **no entry for the packet's Power-Tools strips** (a separate packet section, "Lexicarry-like pictures for power-tools," phase1.txt line 1573) even though functionally they overlap Key frames 4/5/8.
