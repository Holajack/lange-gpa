# GPA Methodology Fidelity Review — Phase 2–6 App Implementation Blueprints

Reviewer: fidelity critic pass, cross-checked against `research/knowledge-base/phase{2..6}.md` and `nurturer-side.md` on the LaCie source (`/Volumes/LaCie/GPA_Language_Learning`). Scope: does the "solo/AI-partner fallback" or "pen-pal" design in each blueprint quietly undermine what makes the phase real GPA, and do the five blueprints compose into one coherent evolving relationship (nurturer → mentor → lifestyle community)?

**Headline finding:** across all five blueprints, the single biggest fidelity risk the task asked me to hunt for — AI quietly substituting for a real host person where the methodology requires one (Phase 4 biography, Phase 5 native-to-native discourse) — does **not** occur. Every blueprint is explicit and consistent that fabricated "AI biography," "AI cultural interpretation," or "AI native-to-native speech" would be a hollow imitation, and each designs an honestly-labeled bridge (rehearsal, drilling on real content, dictionary lookup vs. elaboration, queueing for a real person) instead. The concerns below are narrower: one deliberate softening of an explicit "hard gate," one missing whole activity, one specific role-reversal bug in an AI rehearsal design, and a couple of smaller cross-referencing gaps.

---

## Phase 2 — Emerging (Story-Building with Pictures)

**Verdict: sound**

Numbers checked against `phase2.md` and all correct: 150h total, 2A=50/2B=75/2C=25, 7–8 words/hour pace target (guide's own evaluation checklist says "at least eight"), the 2C escape hatch ("postpone to Phase 3, extend 2B by 25h") reproduced verbatim as a literal UI button, and the "2 to 4 GPs per nurturer... solo GP needs more picture books because all the creativity demand falls on one person" line lifted correctly into §3.3.

The AI-substitution reasoning is unusually careful for this phase specifically because 2A's nurturer function is genuinely "mechanical and content-free" per the guide (2A: "reactive helper, zero prep, recasting the GP's broken output" — matches `phase2.md`'s own "responsive role — zero preparation required" framing), so letting Nuri run 2A's describe-and-recast loop is a legitimate reading of the source, not an overreach. Everywhere the activity turns on a real person's real cultural lens (2B's nurturer misreadings, 2C life-story reciprocity, 2C conversation-observation) the blueprint says NO outright and doesn't hedge. The pen-pal design (§4) is explicitly built from "named, phase-authentic rituals" (the guide's literal daily "one new fact" ritual, the "all-in-one picture" persistent canvas) rather than open chat, and §4.5 explicitly guards against the debrief-should-trend-to-zero and anti-English-chitchat rules the guide states for this phase.

No concerns rise to the level of worth reporting.

## Phase 3 — Becoming Knowable ("Stuff-We-Both-Know")

**Verdict: minor-concerns**

This blueprint is the most rigorous of the five about *correcting* the app's existing methodological bugs (restores non-inverted massaging, restores the Action Cartoon information gap, keeps the 3A/3B/3C hour split at 100/75/75 instead of the app's wrong numbers) and is the only blueprint that explicitly names and defuses the exact plateau/chitchat risk the task asked me to hunt for (§4.5: "The guide is explicit that debriefing... should trend toward zero across Phase 2, and that small talk 'arises out of activities' rather than replacing them... deliberately built from named, phase-authentic rituals... so it can't quietly become an English-language social app riding on top of a language-learning one").

1. **The 3B→3C hard gate is deliberately softened for MVP, in tension with the guide's own "HARD GATE" language.** `phase3.md` line 293: *"HARD GATE: 'Consider that you do not graduate from Phase 3B to 3C until you have done this!'"* (new relationships beyond the paid nurturer, 3–5 countable social hrs/week). The blueprint's §1.10 ships this as "a soft checklist item... not a hard block," and the MVP cut (§6) explicitly defers "hard enforcement of the 3B→3C social gate" with no stated path back to enforcing it. This is transparently reasoned (a real product would rather not lock users out on a self-reported metric), but it is a direct, acknowledged departure from an explicit "hard gate" instruction in the source guide, and the blueprint doesn't propose *any* eventual mechanism (soft-gate-that-later-hardens, a check-in nudge that escalates, etc.) — it just defers indefinitely. Worth a product decision, not silently inherited as a given.

2. **Bridge Story's AI-legitimacy reasoning is sound but slightly under-argued as a *relationship* matter.** The blueprint correctly notes Bridge Stories are drawn from "a third world... neither party's world" and uses that to justify AI narration as non-hollow (§2). That's a fair reading of the guide's own rationale for *why* Bridge Stories require no host background — but the blueprint doesn't explicitly flag that Bridge Stories were therefore never a relationship-building activity to begin with (unlike Host Stories, Reminiscing, Shared Experience, all correctly gated NO), so an AI carrying "≥half of session time" of the phase's content doesn't erode Phase 3's "Becoming Knowable" relationship goal the way it would look like it should at first glance. This is stated correctly in effect but not made explicit as a defense against the obvious objection ("you're letting AI eat the phase's main activity") — a reader skimming just the summary table could reasonably worry this undermines Phase 3's actual purpose.

## Phase 4 — Deep Personal Relationships

**Verdict: minor-concerns**

This is the phase where the "AI cannot substitute for a real host person's biography" risk is most acute, and the blueprint handles it correctly and without hedging: "Life Story Activity — NO, cannot be substituted... Having the AI 'tell its own life story'... would fabricate exactly the content the activity exists to make real, and would train the grower on falsehoods (worse than nothing...)." Confidentiality design (physician/lawyer/priest-grade promise, instant erase control on the mentor's — i.e., the storyteller's — side) is grounded correctly in `phase4.md`'s own language and correctly targets the mentor as the vulnerable disclosing party (confirmed against the source: the mentor is the Life Story teller, the grower is the listener/recorder).

1. **The pen-pal "retelling audience" item doesn't carry forward the anonymization requirement.** §1.8 (core features) correctly captures the guide's rule that mentors' stories may only be retold "non-confidential[ly], anonymized... to friends, co-workers, taxi drivers" — but §4 item 5 ("Retelling audience... a short retelling voice note sent to a second contact counts toward the multi-telling improvement") doesn't cross-reference that constraint at the specific touchpoint where a grower might retell a *mentor's* confidential life story into the pen-pal thread. The data-model consent note in §4 covers *sharing an existing clip* onward, but a *freshly recorded retelling* of someone else's disclosed story is a different action that isn't structurally reminded to stay anonymized/non-identifying at the point of recording.

2. **No encoded equivalent of the guide's own readiness/fallback rule for entering Phase 4.** `phase4.md` §"Entry gate/readiness test" is explicit: if the Life Story Activity "is exceedingly hard the first day or two, press on... but if after a week or two it doesn't get any easier, then you should spend more time with Phase 2 and 3 activities... Then try returning to Phase 4 Activities after a month or two." Phase 2's blueprint sets a real precedent for this exact pattern — the 2C "postpone, extend 2B by 25h" escape hatch is explicitly built as "a literal button... not something buried in settings." Phase 4's "sink-or-swim" difficulty (the blueprint's own framing) makes this safety valve arguably *more* important than Phase 2's, yet no equivalent surfaces anywhere in §1–§6 of the Phase 4 blueprint.

3. **Two-Recorder Technique is referenced only as deferred scope, never described.** It's named in the MVP cut as explicitly deferred and referenced by Phase 2's and Phase 5's dependency sections as shared-infra precedent, but Phase 4's own §1 core-feature-mapping never describes what it actually is (per `phase4.md` §C, it's an *alternative workflow* for Activities 1–2 adopted only once the step-by-step forms are "second nature" — not a separate activity). Minor completeness gap, not a fidelity violation, since deferring an advanced alternative workflow is defensible — but a reader of the blueprint alone can't tell what's being deferred.

## Phase 5 — Widening Understanding (native-to-native discourse)

**Verdict: minor-concerns**

This blueprint is the strongest of the five on the exact question the task foregrounds — "AI substituting for something that must be a real host person." §2's "Cannot authentically substitute" section on collecting native-to-native recordings is unequivocal and well-argued (the GP-absence rule, the risk of "synthetic content masquerading as ethnographic reality," the mentor's elaboration requiring "lived membership in the host culture that an AI does not have and cannot honestly claim to have"). The clarify-loop AI bridge is careful to label a dictionary lookup as *not* a mentor's contextual elaboration, specifically so growers don't mistake a flat definition for the real thing. Numbers (500h at hours 1000–1500, 100–125h clarify target, 3:1 ratio, 20-min recording minimum, GP-absence rule, 10,000-word target, 100–200h social-visiting cap) all check out against `phase5.md`.

1. **Phase 5's own reading programme is entirely missing from the blueprint, and the gap is a known, named phase misplacement.** `phase5.md` line 358 states plainly: *"The reading programme is missing from Phase 5... Phase 5 'is the point at which you can really get into heavy-duty reading'; solo reading with a dictionary at home; mentor reads aloud in meetings, recorded and clarified; never GP-reads-to-mentor... The app moved all of this to Phase 6 (`p6-read`)... a genuine phase misplacement relative to the source (Phase 6's guide continues it, but it starts in Phase 5)."* The Phase 5 blueprint's §1 core-feature-mapping has no reading activity at all (its 7 mapped activities are record/tensteps/retell/library/media/epic/community), and the Phase 6 blueprint's §1.7 "Extensive Reading + Recorded Word Discussion" presents the same mechanic as if it originates in Phase 6, with no note that it should already be underway from Phase 5. Every other place these blueprints hit an app-vs-guide delta, they explicitly design against the guide and flag the correction (Phase 3's massaging inversion, Phase 4's mentor-vs-nurturer terminology, Phase 3B/C hours) — this is the one place a documented delta was inherited silently rather than corrected. Given the KB traces this explicitly, it's a fixable, concrete omission rather than a debatable judgment call.

## Phase 6 — Ever Participating, Ever Growing / Self-Sustaining Growth

**Verdict: minor-concerns**

Numbers check out cleanly against `phase6.md` (100/300-hour meter with the 5 named characteristics and the explicit exclusion of "the same sort of unstructured conversation... with the house helper," the 10–20h Stage-A gate, the 10%/20% waking-hours benchmark, the Needs Analysis scoring worked example). The AI-boundary reasoning correctly refuses to let Nuri originate Scripts of Life, Stage-B modeling, or the Record-Yourself-for-Feedback "would a host person say this" judgment, with the same non-hedging tone as Phases 4–5. The pen-pal design explicitly rules out "AI-generated messages inserted into these threads under any circumstances... which would directly violate the 'not a hollow imitation' constraint" (§4.6) — the strongest statement of this principle across all five blueprints.

1. **The AI role-play rehearsal bridge appears to reverse the roles the guide specifies, which would make it rehearse the wrong skill.** `phase6.md` line 83 is explicit: *"have your nurturer pretend she is you, and you are the beautician. She acts out the entire visit... You can then switch roles."* — the **partner** plays the customer/grower, and the **grower** plays the host professional (this is the entire point: the grower practices producing professional-register language, the actual growth target for the discourse track). The blueprint's §2 AI bridge has it backwards: *"Nuri plays a fixed, clearly-artificial 'beautician'/'mechanic' character... so the grower can rehearse the shape of the exchange before doing the real role-play."* If Nuri plays the beautician, the grower is cast as the *customer* in the rehearsal — the easier, more-familiar role, and not the one the real exercise (and the domain-vocabulary Prelude that's supposed to feed it) is built to develop. As written, this rehearsal doesn't actually warm up the skill it claims to warm up. This looks like a straightforward fix (Nuri should play the *customer* so the grower rehearses playing the professional) rather than a deep design problem, but as specified it's a genuine mismatch with the source mechanic.

2. **Inherits the Phase 5 reading-programme misplacement without flagging it** (see Phase 5 §1 above) — §1.7 presents "Extensive Reading" as native to Phase 6 with no note that the guide has it beginning in Phase 5 and continuing here. Not this blueprint's own bug (it's downstream of Phase 5's), but worth fixing in the same pass since both blueprints exist together.

## Cross-blueprint composition check (nurturer → mentor → lifestyle community)

The five blueprints compose consistently:
- Helper terminology is correctly sequenced and never contradicted: "nurturer" (paid, trained) through Phases 1–3, renaming to "mentor" (paid or exchange, **untrained**) starting Phase 4, continuing as "mentor" through Phase 5, then explicitly becoming plural/lifestyle-embedded (discourse partner, expert, reading-club partner, coworkers) at Phase 6 — each blueprint states the correct qualifying signal for its phase (method-training+quiz for 1–3; willingness+trustworthiness for 4–5; eloquence/subject-matter expertise, no training, for 6) and Phase 6's own blueprint includes a table comparing all three tiers side by side, which is an accurate summary of the other four blueprints' claims.
- Continuity is modeled consistently: Phase 2 defaults to "continue with your Phase 1 nurturer," Phase 4 defaults to "continue with your existing nurturer, relabeled mentor" and explicitly cites the guide's own preference for this path, and neither later phase invents a contradictory sourcing default.
- Shared infrastructure (Listening Library, Word Log, async threading, certification-module framework, wallet/credits) is proposed once and consistently referenced as "build once, not per-phase" across all five documents, without contradictory schemas — each phase's dependency section builds on, rather than restates or contradicts, the prior phase's version of the same primitive.
- Payment structure evolves consistently and matches the source: paid/exchange dual-rail through Phases 1–3 and largely through 4–5 (Life Story continuation can be either; Walk-of-Life is explicitly paid per the guide), shifting to a genuinely mixed unpaid/one-off-paid/exchange model at Phase 6 — no blueprint contradicts another on this axis.

No contradictions were found in what the helper role *is* at a given phase — the one weakness in the cross-phase story is the Phase 5→6 reading-programme handoff being silently dropped rather than carried forward (noted under both phases above), which is a completeness gap in the composition rather than a contradiction.
