# Phase 2–3 Story Library (authentic GPA story progression)

Source: Thomson "Phase 2 Storybooks" bibliography + the 32-item wordless-book scan
folder + Phase 3 "Stuff-We-Both-Know" guidance. The actual scans are copyrighted
(Mercer Mayer, Fernando Krahn, Jeannie Baker, John Goodall, etc.) and stay in the
owner's Dropbox — this catalog is the curation/metadata the app's Story Library
feature will use. **Method rule:** if a book has text, cover every word — the
PICTURES must tell the story (image + the nurturer's voice, never translation).

## How stories are used by phase
- **Phase 2 (Story Builder):** the nurturer narrates a wordless picture book while
  the grower listens & points; later the grower retells. Move to the next tier when
  the GP gets fewer than ~7–8 new words/hour from the current set.
- **Phase 3 (In-Sider / "Stuff-We-Both-Know"):** shared stories both already know —
  fairy tales (Cinderella), religious stories (David & Goliath), folk tales,
  biographies; plus WORDLESS animation: Tom & Jerry, Mr. Bean, and Max7.org
  "No Language" animations. The grower and nurturer discuss a story they both hold.

## App feature implication (future, Phase 2/3 build)
A **Story Library** screen: tiered shelves (Introductory→Advanced), each story card =
cover + tier + star rating + "phase fit" + a "no words — the pictures tell it" badge.
For licensed/owned scans, a wordless-story player (page-turner, nurturer records
narration per spread → becomes the grower's re-living audio, same as the Talking
Picture Dictionary). For unlicensed titles, show as "bring this book" suggestions +
link the public-domain / Max7.org "No Language" animations and Tom&Jerry/Mr.Bean as
streamable Phase-3 shared-story material. Lexicarry-like strips (empty speech bubbles)
power the social-expression "who is saying…?" pointing game from the graphics packet.
